IPO Saathi Privacy Policy

IPO Saathi · Staycold Solutions

Effective date: 27 November 2026

IPO Saathi ("the app") is developed and published by Staycold Solutions ("we", "us"). The app shows information about Indian IPOs and lets you check IPO allotment status. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • There is no account, sign-up or login.
  • PAN numbers you save stay on your device, encrypted.
  • A PAN is sent only to the IPO's registrar, and only when you tap Check.
  • The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which screens and features are used. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • We do not sell your data, and the app shows no ads.

Data the app handles

PAN numbers and labels. You can save PANs with a label such as "Self" or "Mom". They are stored only on your device, in encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). They never leave your device except as described below.

Allotment checks. When you check allotment, the app sends the PAN directly from your phone to the official registrar for that IPO over HTTPS. Registrars include KFin Technologies, MUFG Intime India, Bigshare Services, Maashitla Securities, Skyline Financial Services, Purva Sharegistry, Cameo Corporate Services, Integrated Registry Management and Beetal Financial. The registrar's response is shown to you. The app saves the outcome (for example "Allotted 1,200 shares") on your device so you can see it later. It does not save the PAN with that result. Each registrar's handling of the request is governed by its own privacy policy.

App settings. Preferences such as theme, default tab and app lock are stored only on your device.

App lock. If you turn on app lock, your device's fingerprint or face unlock is used. Biometric data is handled by your phone's operating system and is never accessible to the app.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings → Suggest us, we receive your message, the type (feature idea, problem or other), the app version and your phone's operating system version. If you choose to add an email address or phone number, we receive it too, and use it only to reply to you. Feedback is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (a Firebase service) and can only be read by Staycold Solutions. To have your feedback deleted, write to the contact address below.

Usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service by Google, to count how the app is used. For example, it records which screens are opened, which IPOs are viewed, and how many PANs were checked and allotted. It also records basic device information such as device model, operating system, app version, language and approximate country or city. This is linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name.

Analytics never receives your PAN numbers, the labels you give them, names returned by registrars, or what you type in search. The app does not collect the advertising ID.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

IPO data

IPO information (GMP, subscription, dates, financials) is downloaded from a public data file. This download does not include any information about you. As with any internet request, the hosting provider (GitHub) may see your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location or advertising ID. We only receive an email or phone number if you add one to your feedback.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete a saved PAN from the PANs screen in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes all data the app stored on your device. To delete feedback you sent, or to ask about any data we hold, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children.

Disclaimer

IPO Saathi is an information app. It is not a broker or a SEBI-registered investment adviser, and it is not affiliated with NSE, BSE, SEBI or any registrar. GMP is an unofficial indicator and is not a guarantee of listing price.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or erase personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent. For analytics, the Settings switch withdraws consent immediately. For anything else, email us.
